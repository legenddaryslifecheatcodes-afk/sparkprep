Every font in this folder is open source under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org),
downloaded from Google Fonts: EB Garamond, Libre Baskerville, Crimson Pro, Lora, Merriweather, Source Serif 4,
Cormorant Garamond, Lato, Playfair Display, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Cinzel.
The OFL allows embedding these fonts in printed and sold books. SparkPrep's Interior Studio embeds them in
the interior PDFs it typesets.
